Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. A plain-language summary of what WanderList does and doesn't do with your data.

The short version

WanderList doesn't have user accounts, doesn't use cookies for tracking, and doesn't sell data to anyone, because it doesn't collect any in the first place beyond what's described below.

What's stored, and where

Your saved trips and packing checklist progress are stored using your browser's local storage, directly on your device. This information never leaves your browser, is not sent to any WanderList server, and is not visible to us in any way. Clearing your browser's site data or using a different browser/device will reset it.

Third-party services

Pages on this site load a small number of external resources needed to render properly:

  • Destination photography served from Wikimedia Commons
  • Web fonts served from Google Fonts
  • The 3D globe on the homepage uses the Three.js library, loaded from a public CDN

These providers may log standard technical request data (such as IP address) as part of normal web hosting. This is governed by their own respective privacy policies, not WanderList's.

Contact form

If you use the contact form, the information you submit is used only to respond to your message. This demo site does not currently route form submissions to a live inbox.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly.

Questions

Reach out via the contact page with anything privacy-related.
